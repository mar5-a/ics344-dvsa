# Team Workflow

Recommended operating rules for a shared project:

1. Assign one owner per lesson.
2. Record AWS resources touched before editing anything.
3. Capture exploit evidence before applying the fix.
4. Avoid editing shared resources for two lessons at the same time.
5. Update `report/LESSON_STATUS.md` after each milestone.
6. Keep raw screenshots and exact commands under the matching lesson folder.
7. Teach the rest of the team how the lesson worked after it is complete.
